# Hackathon (CompPhysHack2027)

CompPhysHack2027, the successor to [CompPhysHack2026](https://qc-hybrid.github.io/CompPhysHack2026/), is held as part of the spring school.

- Dates: daytime on March 1–3 (9:00–12:00 and 13:20–16:20). February 28 is common to all participants, with no hackathon
- Venue: the main hall of Okinawa Ken Seinen Kaikan
- Language: English
- Themes: to be announced

## Format (planned)

March 1 starts with an introduction in lecture format. Later that day, participants split into teams and work on hands-on exercises, which continue until March 3.

## How to join

- Choose the hackathon when you register for the school. Only registered participants can join.
- The number of places is limited, first come, first served.
- Please apply only if you can focus on the hackathon for all three days. If there is a lecture you want to attend, you may leave to join it. We do not recommend it, but it is fine at your own responsibility.
- Participants who do not join the hackathon attend the [lectures](/en/lectures) at the same time.
