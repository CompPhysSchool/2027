# Hackathon (CompPhysHack2027)

CompPhysHack2027, the successor to [CompPhysHack2026](https://qc-hybrid.github.io/CompPhysHack2026/), is held as part of the spring school.

::: warning Provisional
The program, lecturers, and other details on this page are provisional and may change without notice. We will update this page as things are decided.
:::

- Dates: daytime on March 1–3 (9:00–12:15 and 13:20–16:20). February 28 is common to all participants, with no hackathon. Attending February 28 is optional for you ([languages on the first day](/en/timetable#languages-on-february-28-first-day))
- Venue: the main hall of Okinawa Ken Seinen Kaikan
- Language: English
- Theme: Agentic coding for computational physics

## Computing resources

Hackathon participants who meet the conditions for using the supercomputers can use [Pegasus](https://www.ccs.tsukuba.ac.jp/eng/supercomputers/#Pegasus) (NVIDIA H100 GPUs) and [Sirius](https://www.ccs.tsukuba.ac.jp/eng/supercomputers/#Sirius) (AMD Instinct MI300A APUs) at the Center for Computational Sciences, University of Tsukuba. Eligibility and how to apply will be announced to registered participants later. You can of course also use your own laptop or your own computing resources.

## How to join

- Choose the hackathon when you register for the school. Only registered participants can join.
- The number of places is limited, first come, first served.
- Please apply only if you can focus on the hackathon for all three days. If there is a lecture you want to attend, you may leave to join it. We do not recommend it, but it is fine at your own responsibility.
- Participants who do not join the hackathon attend the [lectures](/en/lectures) at the same time.
- If you attend February 28 and do not understand Japanese, we recommend the English group seminar and the poster session. The opening and the session on using AI in research are spoken in Japanese with English slides.
