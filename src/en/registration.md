# Registration

Registration is now open. Please register using the form below.

::: tip Two tracks: Lectures or Hackathon
On March 1–3, the program is split into two tracks: **[Lectures](/en/lectures)** and **[Hackathon (CompPhysHack2027)](/en/hackathon)**.

- **If you want to join the hackathon, you must explicitly select it in the registration form** (only registered participants can join).
- Hackathon participants are expected to focus on the hackathon for all three days; leaving to attend parts of the lectures is allowed at your own responsibility (though not recommended).
:::

There are separate forms for in-person and online participation. Use the one that matches how you will attend.

- The deadline for in-person participation (including the hackathon and poster presentations) is **January 31 (Sun), 2027**. Places are first come, first served, and may close earlier when they are full.
- Online registration stays open until just before the school.
- Participation is free, both in person and online. The banquet is planned to be paid in advance by bank transfer; we will announce the amount and account once they are decided.
- Please arrange your own accommodation. The organizers do not arrange accommodation.
- Travel support has not been decided, and it may not be offered. Please register without counting on it. If it is offered, we will let registrants know.
- In-person participants are asked whether they will join the group seminar on February 28, and if so, whether they would like an English group. You may choose an English group even if you understand Japanese ([languages on the first day](/en/timetable#languages-on-february-28-first-day)).
- If you are traveling from abroad and need visa support, please see the [Visa](/en/visa) page.
- How we handle the information you give us is in the [privacy policy](/privacy).

For questions, see the [FAQ](/en/faq) or write to [compphysschool2027@googlegroups.com](mailto:compphysschool2027@googlegroups.com).
