# Registration

Registration is scheduled to open on **November 20, 2026**. The form will be posted on this page.

- One form covers both in-person and online participation.
- Places are first come, first served. In-person participation, the hackathon, and poster presentations close when they are full.
- Participation is free, both in person and online. The banquet is planned to be paid; we will announce the amount once it is decided.
- Travel support for students is under consideration. We do not ask about it in the form; we will contact applicants once it is decided.
- You choose whether to join the hackathon when you register. See the [Hackathon](/en/hackathon) page.
- How we handle the information you give us is in the [privacy policy](/privacy).

For questions, see the [FAQ](/en/faq) or write to [compphysschool2027@googlegroups.com](mailto:compphysschool2027@googlegroups.com).
