# Visa

If you are attending in person from abroad and need visa support to enter Japan, please apply through our dedicated visa support request form.

::: info Request form coming soon
The visa support request form is currently being prepared. We will post the link on this page as soon as it is ready. Please submit your request through that form once it becomes available.
:::

- Please complete your [registration](/en/registration) first.
- If your request is urgent before the form opens, or if you have any questions, please contact [compphysschool2027@googlegroups.com](mailto:compphysschool2027@googlegroups.com).
