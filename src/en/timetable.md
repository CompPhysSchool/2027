# Provisional schedule

::: warning This schedule is provisional
Times and session names may change. Lecture topics and speakers are not yet decided.
:::

Registration is scheduled to open on November 20, 2026.

The school runs from February 28 (Sun) to March 4 (Thu), 2027, at [Okinawa Ken Seinen Kaikan](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WWgnRZxDTBfwh7Mc8) in Naha. From Naha Airport Station, Yui Rail takes about 10 minutes to Asahibashi Station, then about a 10-minute walk.

CompPhysHack2027, the successor to [CompPhysHack2026](https://qc-hybrid.github.io/CompPhysHack2026/), is held alongside the school. From the second day (March 1), a lecture course and a hackathon course run in parallel. There is no hackathon on the first day. The hackathon is in English. On the afternoon of February 28, there are topic introductions on using AI in research, followed by a panel discussion. The talks are in Japanese, with slides in English. The lecture course is in Japanese. The opening, group seminar, company sessions, and the general meeting and closing ceremony are also in Japanese. Poster sessions are in Japanese and English, and English posters are encouraged. A lecture given in English will be marked on its own.
