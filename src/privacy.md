# Privacy policy

This policy explains how the organizers of the Computational Physics Spring School 2027 (the "School") handle the personal information you give us when you register.

## Who handles your information

The organizing committee of the School. Registration data is managed by the organizers and their administrative staff at Saitama University. We handle personal information in line with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan.

## What we collect

What you enter in the registration form, for example:

- Name, email address, and phone number
- Affiliation and position, and for students, year and supervisor
- Research field and technical background
- How you will take part (in person or online), and your choices for the hackathon, poster session, company roundtable, and banquet
- Food allergies, accessibility needs, and other notes you choose to give us

## How we use it

- To run the School: confirming registration, contacting you, preparing name lists, rooms, and meals
- To arrange travel support and accommodation, if these apply to you
- To contact you in an emergency during the School
- To make statistics without names (for example, numbers of participants by position or field) for reports to our funding programs and sponsors, and to plan future schools

## Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only as follows:

- **Companies at the company roundtable:** only if you agree to this in the registration form, for their recruitment and similar purposes
- **The venue and caterers:** only what they need, such as food allergies for the meals
- **Saitama University and the funding programs:** what is needed for accounting, for example for travel support
- **When required by law**

## Use of AI tools

We may use AI tools to help process registration data, for example to sort or count responses. We only use AI services under zero data retention terms: the service does not store what we send or what it returns, and does not use it to train models.

## Storage and retention

Registration data is stored in Saitama University's Microsoft 365 environment. We delete personal information within one year after the School ends, except records that university accounting rules require us to keep, which we keep for the period those rules require. Statistics without names may be kept.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete your information, or withdraw your consent to sharing with companies, by writing to [compphysschool2027@googlegroups.com](mailto:compphysschool2027@googlegroups.com).

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and note the date.
